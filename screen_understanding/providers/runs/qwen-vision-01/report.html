ModelRun labels were broadly correct across the three repeats, with “Manage tabs” less specific than “Tab overview.” Cerebras consistently mislabeled sidebar and tab overview, and named only one direction for the paired navigation control. The same public model is being served with different endpoint settings: metadata lists ModelRun FP4, Cerebras FP16, DeepInfra BF16. This experiment cannot isolate why their outputs differ.
